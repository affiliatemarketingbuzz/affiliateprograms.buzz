Campaign Details: Commission, Cookie Window, and Tracking

Understanding the Coursera affiliate program structure helps you plan content and set realistic earning expectations.

Commission Structure

  • Payout: 7% flat commission on every successful sale — both one-time course purchases and subscription plans like Coursera Plus.
  • The 7% rate is consistent across all product types, making earnings calculation straightforward. A Coursera Plus annual subscription at ₹7,999 earns approximately ₹560 per conversion. Scale that across consistent content traffic and the numbers add up meaningfully.

Cookie Duration

  • Cookie window: 30 days — when someone clicks your affiliate link, you receive credit for any purchase they make within the next 30 days. Online learning purchases are often considered decisions — learners browse courses, compare options, and return days later to subscribe. A 30-day cookie captures these delayed conversions.

Tracking and Platform Support

  • Tracking: Real-time — monitor clicks, conversions, and pending commissions as they happen through your affiliate dashboard.
  • Platform support: Web and mobile web — conversions from both desktop and mobile browsers are tracked and credited.
  • Deeplink support: Available — create affiliate links pointing directly to specific courses, certificate programs, or the Coursera Plus pricing page rather than just the homepage. Deep links that take users directly to the course they're interested in convert significantly better than generic homepage links.
  • Geo: Worldwide — the campaign accepts traffic from any country, making it suitable for affiliates with international audiences.

Validation Period

  • Validation: 55 days — commissions are verified before payout to confirm legitimate conversions. This standard validation window accounts for refunds, payment verification, and subscription confirmation.

Allowed and Restricted Promotion Methods

The Coursera campaign permits several promotional channels while restricting others. Understanding these boundaries prevents wasted effort and compliance issues.

What's Allowed

  • Content marketing (blog posts, course reviews, comparison guides, career advice articles)
  • Coupon and deal sites
  • Cashback platforms
  • Telegram channels and communities
  • Influencer marketing (YouTube reviews, Instagram content, TikTok)
  • Native media buying (sponsored content on discovery platforms)
  • Display advertising (banner campaigns on relevant websites)
  • Incentive-based promotion

This range gives education affiliates across different traffic types clear paths to earning without running into restrictions.

What's Restricted

  • Google Search ads (PPC) — not allowed
  • Social media ads (Facebook, Instagram paid campaigns) — not allowed
  • Email marketing — not allowed
  • SMS campaigns — not allowed
  • Push notifications — not allowed
  • Pop traffic — not allowed
  • Mobile app traffic — not allowed
  • Brand keyword bidding — strictly prohibited

These restrictions mean your promotional strategy should focus on organic content, coupon/cashback platforms, Telegram, influencer channels, and permitted display/native advertising. PPC affiliates who rely primarily on Google Ads will need to use other traffic generation methods for this campaign.

How to Join the Coursera Campaign Step by Step

Getting started with the Coursera affiliate program is a straightforward process.

Step 1: Create Your Affiliate Account

Sign up as an affiliate publisher on the network hosting the Coursera campaign. Complete your profile with your traffic sources — blog, YouTube channel, Telegram community, or advertising platforms.

Step 2: Get Approved and Apply for Coursera

Once your account is reviewed and approved, access your affiliate dashboard. Search for the Coursera campaign and submit your application.

Step 3: Generate Tracking and Deep Links

After campaign approval, generate your unique affiliate tracking links. Use deeplink support to create links pointing directly to specific courses, certificate programs, or the Coursera Plus pricing page. Course-specific deep links convert significantly better than generic homepage links.

Step 4: Create and Publish Content

Build content around Coursera offers, course reviews, and career advice that naturally incorporates your affiliate links. Focus on the content formats and channels that match your audience.

Step 5: Track Performance and Optimize

Monitor clicks, conversions, and earnings through the real-time dashboard. Identify which courses, content pieces, and traffic sources drive the strongest conversion rates. Double down on what works and refine what doesn't.

The same dashboard gives you access to campaigns from other renowned brands across travel, e-commerce, education, utility, and finance — enabling you to diversify your affiliate income beyond a single program. A dedicated account manager is also available to help you choose the right campaigns and optimize performance based on what works best for your portfolio.

Whether you're an independent publisher or working with an established affiliate network, the Coursera campaign provides the tracking infrastructure, commission transparency, and product demand that make education affiliate promotions both sustainable and scalable.
